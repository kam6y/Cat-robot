## Findings

E1 [V mechanism; cheapest alternative not behavior-preserving] Conflicts with a recorded design decision. The camera session is stopped and rebuilt for every routed utterance, including utterances the classifier rejects. Location: `ConversationCamera.swift:92-98` (attention toggle), `:177` (reconcile guard), `:187-192` (`stopPhysical`); triggers `ConversationViewModel.swift:982` (`.classifying`), `:1086` (`.thinking`); re-entry `:750` (`startCapture` → `.listening`). Every `.accept`/`.classify` route leaves `.listening` (only `.ignore`/`.dismissPending` stay). Without another holder, `source.stop()` → `AppleFaceCaptureSource.stopOnQueue` (blocking `stopRunning`, :174-185), then on return `configure()` (new AVCaptureSession/DeviceInput, format scan, lockForConfiguration, output, RotationCoordinator, :187-264) + blocking `startRunning()` (:132). Pinned by the diff's own `ConversationCameraTests.testListeningCapturesWithoutTrackingOrLook` (starts=1 on listening, stops=1 on thinking). Who pays: every session without a motion-ready robot (tracking needs `linkReady`, FaceTrackingCoordinator :141-142), i.e. the no-hardware case the README advertises. Worst case: room with TV/other people → each overheard sentence does listening→classifying→notAddressed→listening with a full session rebuild and identical end state; round trip lasts only the classifier call. Cost: per routed utterance one stop+configure+start on the serial faceCapture queue (off main actor); plus loss of warm frames (no facing evidence until first new frame + Vision). Alternatives: (a) demand = `environmentPermitsCapture` (zero restarts; camera stays on through classifying/clarifying/thinking/speaking — what the decision avoided; tracking-on robot-ready sessions already pay this); (b) extend demand through `.classifying` only — removes the restart for rejected / correcting utterances while still releasing the camera during thinking/speaking, so the stated rationale holds though the literal "listening only" changes; (c) short linger timer before stopping attention-only capture (adds a Task); (d) outside diff: reuse the configured AVCaptureSession across stop/start in AppleFaceCaptureSource (removes allocation/config, keeps camera-off policy, not the startRunning cost). Risk: (b)/(c) must move the attention reset (`clearAttention` + `attentionStartedAt = now`) to a separate listening-entry edge or `testLeavingListeningRequiresFreshObservation…` and "late old frame cannot revive" break; (a) changes when the camera is on.

E2 [V] Conflicts with a recorded design decision. On look turns a warm session is torn down ~0.1 s before the look rebuilds it from cold. `LookIntegrationTests.swift:69-73` now expects starts=1/stops=1 at look begin, starts=2 after the 0.1 s look delay (`LookCoordinator.swift:44-49`). listening→thinking stops the attention session; `captureSnapshot` starts a new physical generation; the look's configure/startRunning queue behind the blocking stopRunning. Cost: one extra stop+rebuild per look turn on attention-only devices; the snapshot may wait on stopRunning if it exceeds 0.1 s (unmeasured). Versus base: base also cold-started look here, so this is wasted teardown + possible serialization, not a large latency regression. Only E1(a) helps (warm session → `fulfillSnapshotIfFresh` answers from `frameCache`).

E3 [V loop; P frequency] Conflicts with a recorded design decision. After a capture failure the camera retries on every listening entry (`ConversationCamera.swift:97`). Under a persistent failure (another app holds the camera so `wasInterrupted` keeps firing, or `configure` throws `unsupportedConfiguration`) each non-ignored utterance repeats configure→start→fail→stop. Reaches tracking-on users too: after a failure FaceTrackingCoordinator sets its own `captureFailed` and calls `camera.stop()`, so the next listening entry restarts the camera for attention alone. Base retried only on tracking-eligibility rising edges, `start(generation:)` or `captureSnapshot`. Cheaper: reset `captureFailed` only on environment edges (scene active, authorization change, thermal recovery, tracking start) or add cooldown/backoff. Risk: slower recovery from a transient failure that clears without an environment edge.

## Considered and ruled out

N1 the two awaited permission requests are not independent (tracker's source is the camera; cache + `permissionTask` dedupe; one OS prompt). N2 `selectFace` at query time is cheaper than per-frame and required for current yaw correction (test uses 14 vs 8.5). N3 `recordAttention` per frame is O(1), guarded by `attentionRequested`. N4 new fields are `@ObservationIgnored` → no Observation/SwiftUI invalidation per frame. N5 `refreshAuthorization()`/double `reconcile()` pre-existing; live `source.authorization` read is one `authorizationStatus` call per routed utterance, needed for revocation (test depends). N6 redundant `clearAttention` calls are two stores. N7 some `hasFreshFacingFace` guards implied by `latestAttention != nil` (simplification question). N8 eager `isFacingScreen()` read is needed for `.arm` vs `.refresh`. N9 composition closure captures only app-lifetime `camera`/`preferences`, no cycle. N10 harness closure captures local `attention`, no cycle. N11 diff removes per-turn work (second route pass, wake-name scan). N12 restarts don't touch the audio session. N13 3 fps + Vision during listening is the feature itself. N14 pbxproj/xcscheme skipped (semantic check: only the added test file).

## Evidence limits

No timing of configure/startRunning/stopRunning, first-frame latency after restart, Vision rev3 per frame, or power/thermal difference; costs are operation counts. [P] A restarted session without a fresh frame by routing time turns a free bypass into a classifier call (paid Haiku on devices without Apple Intelligence), mostly after rejection-triggered restarts. [P] At steady state 1/3 s frame interval + Vision + main-actor hop leaves a small margin under 0.4 s: if processing exceeds ~67 ms some routing reads see a stale frame and fall back to the classifier (unmeasured). Persistent-failure frequency and camera-indicator flicker need device observation. No builds/tests/devices run.
